    Biology Lab

    Four ideas that explain how a healthy leaf works, and what changes when it is stressed.

    Photosynthesis

    Green plants use light energy, carbon dioxide and water to make glucose (food) and release oxygen. Chlorophyll in the leaf captures the light.

    6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

    Simplified equation. It happens in chloroplasts, in the presence of light.

    Chlorophyll

    This green pigment absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects green, so leaves look green. If chlorophyll is lost, the leaf can turn yellow and make less food.

    Transpiration

    Water evaporates from leaves through tiny pores called stomata. This loss helps pull water upward from the roots through the stem and also cools the leaf.

    Plant stress

    Too little or too much water, poor light, missing nutrients, pests and disease-causing microbes can all strain a plant. Different stresses can look alike, so context matters.
